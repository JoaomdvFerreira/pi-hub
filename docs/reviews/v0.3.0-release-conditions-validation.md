## Risk assessment

The final score is **32/100 (Yellow)**. The primary remaining risks are the
accepted absence of a fresh clean-install exercise and a full v0.2.0-to-v0.3.0
updater migration rehearsal, plus the deliberately deferred hardening expected
for broader external distribution. These are mitigated for the current profile by
completed live Raspberry Pi validation, canonical automated validation, bounded
device identity filtering, updater signing capability, and the personal-use
operating scope.

Personal-use release is appropriate because the product behavior exercised by the
release conditions is validated. Before broader external distribution, reintroduce
clean-install and updater-migration validation on disposable machines, finalize
distribution and signing hardening, and establish repeatable external-release
operational controls.

## Tooling hygiene

ESLint excludes `.worktrees/**`. This prevents a main-checkout lint run from
traversing linked worktrees and interpreting their TypeScript files through the
wrong TSConfig root; it does not lint every worktree as part of this checkout.

## Publication boundary

No tag, GitHub Release, artifact upload, installer rebuild, or merge to `main`
was performed by this closure. Existing `0.3.0` artifacts predate the final
Storage and Activity fixes and are not publishable. Final installers and updater
signatures must be rebuilt from the final merged release commit before publication.

## Final decision

**READY FOR PERSONAL-USE RELEASE**

Operator actions before publication are limited to review and merge of the
release-readiness changes; rebuilding final `0.3.0` artifacts from final merged
code; verifying the generated `.msi`, `.exe`, and `.sig` artifacts; and explicit
operator authorization for tag/release publication.
